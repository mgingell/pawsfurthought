<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Training and costs</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>Where do the dogs come from?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Every dog is trained and certified by <a href="https://www.caresks.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CARES, Inc.</a> (Canine Assistance, Rehabilitation and Education Services) in Concordia, Kansas. CARES has placed nearly 2,000 assistance dogs since 1993, and Paws Fur Thought represents CARES for pairing service dogs with veterans in Atlantic Canada.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>What does the training involve?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>You'll attend a mandatory 5-day training session with your service dog at CARES in Concordia, Kansas. You learn to work with your dog as a team before you bring them home.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>What does it cost me?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Paws Fur Thought funds the cost of the service dog, your flights, and your accommodation in Concordia. You'll need to rent a car to drive from the Wichita airport to Concordia, about 2 hours and 15 minutes. [CONFIRM: is the rental car reimbursed? Are meals covered?]</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:heading -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">About service dogs</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>Is a service dog the same as an emotional support animal?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No. A service dog is trained and tested for public access <em>and</em> task-trained to help their handler with a specific disability, such as OSI/PTSD intervention. In Nova Scotia, service dogs are covered by the <a href="https://novascotia.ca/servicedogs/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Service Dog Act</a>.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
<!-- /wp:details -->

<!-- wp:details -->
<details class="wp-block-details"><summary>Can I pet a service dog I see in public?</summary><!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Please don't pet, call, or feed a working service dog without asking the handler first. The dog is on the job, and distractions can make it harder for them to help their handler.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></details>
