Who We Are

Paws Fur Thought is a not-for-profit organization that pairs Canadian Military and RCMP veterans and serving members diagnosed with an Operational Stress Injury (OSI) or PTSD with fully trained, certified service dogs.

We are proud to be a recognized service dog provider in the Wounded Warriors Canada Service Dog Program, and we coordinate the pairing of veterans with dogs trained and certified by CARES, Inc.

What Is a Service Dog?

There is often confusion about what makes a true service dog. Every service dog is trained and tested for public access and task-trained to help their handler with a specific disability. That can include diabetic and seizure alerting, mobility assistance, autism support, hearing assistance, and OSI/PTSD intervention.

In Nova Scotia, service dogs and their handlers are protected under the Service Dog Act.

How It Works

Apply

Complete our online intake form. It helps us confirm your eligibility.

Hear back

If you're accepted, we'll contact you within a week to continue the process.

Train together

Spend 5 days training with your new service dog at CARES in Concordia, Kansas. Training is mandatory for new and successor dogs.

Go home as a team

Return home with a fully trained, certified service dog, with Paws Fur Thought behind you.

What's covered

Paws Fur Thought funds the cost of your service dog, your flights, and your accommodation in Concordia. You'll need to rent a car for the drive from the Wichita airport to Concordia (about 2 hours 15 minutes).

Our Partners

CARES, Inc. (Canine Assistance, Rehabilitation and Education Services) in Concordia, Kansas, trains and certifies every dog we place. CARES has placed nearly 2,000 assistance dogs since 1993.

Wounded Warriors Canada supports Paws Fur Thought through its Service Dog Program. Your donations to WWC help fund the dogs, training and travel for our veterans.

Help Pair a Veteran with a Service Dog

Paws Fur Thought is a not-for-profit supported by Wounded Warriors Canada. Every donation helps cover a service dog, training, and travel for a Canadian veteran.
