Donate

Help pair a Canadian veteran with a fully trained, certified service dog.

Paws Fur Thought is a not-for-profit supported by Wounded Warriors Canada (WWC). Donations are made through WWC, a registered Canadian charity.

How to donate

Go to WWC

Open the Wounded Warriors Canada donation page.

Choose the program

Pick PTSD Service Dogs as the designation for your gift.

Add a note

Write “For Paws Fur Thought” in the comment or message box.

Complete your gift

Finish the donation on the WWC site. Thank you!

Donate through Wounded Warriors Canada

Where your donation goes

Donations help Paws Fur Thought pair Canadian veterans and serving members with fully trained, certified service dogs. We cover the cost of the dog, flights, and accommodation for the mandatory 5-day training in Concordia, Kansas.

Questions about giving, or interested in fundraising for us? Contact us.
