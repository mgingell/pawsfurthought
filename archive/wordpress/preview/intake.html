Apply for a Service Dog

For Canadian Military and RCMP veterans and serving members in Atlantic Canada living with an Operational Stress Injury (OSI) or PTSD.

Our online intake form will be available in late October 2026. Please check back soon. For questions about successor dogs or general inquiries, email info.pawsfurthought@gmail.com.

Who can apply

  • Canadian Armed Forces and RCMP veterans and serving members
  • Diagnosed with an Operational Stress Injury (OSI) or PTSD as a result of military or policing service
  • Living in Atlantic Canada (Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, or Newfoundland and Labrador)

What to expect

Apply

Complete the intake form. It helps us confirm your eligibility.

Hear back

If you're accepted, we'll contact you within a week to continue the process.

Train together

Attend a mandatory 5-day training session with your dog at CARES in Concordia, Kansas. This applies to new and successor dogs.

What's covered

Paws Fur Thought funds the cost of your service dog, your flights, and your accommodation in Concordia. You'll need to rent a car for the drive from the Wichita airport to Concordia (about 2 hours 15 minutes).
