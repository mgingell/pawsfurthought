Donate

Help pair a Canadian veteran with a fully trained, certified service dog.

Paws Fur Thought is a small, volunteer-led organization. We don't handle donations ourselves. Our partner Wounded Warriors Canada (WWC), a registered Canadian charity, looks after the financial and charitable side of our program.

That means every gift for Paws Fur Thought is made through WWC, while our volunteers focus on placing service dogs with veterans.

How to donate

Go to WWC

Open the Wounded Warriors Canada donation page.

Select a designation

Pick Paws Fur Thought as the designation for your gift.

Complete your gift

Finish the donation on the WWC site. Thank you!

Donate through Wounded Warriors Canada

Where your donation goes

Donations help Paws Fur Thought pair Canadian veterans and serving members with fully trained, certified service dogs. We cover the cost of the dog, flights, accommodation and rental vehicles for the mandatory 5-day training in Concordia, Kansas, and a volunteer escorts each group of new handlers.
