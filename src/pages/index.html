Who We Are

Paws Fur Thought is a small, volunteer-led organization that coordinates the placement of fully trained, certified service dogs with Canadian Military and RCMP veterans and serving members diagnosed with an Operational Stress Injury (OSI) or PTSD.

We work alongside two partners. Our dogs are trained and certified by CARES, Inc., and we are proud to be a recognized provider in the Wounded Warriors Canada Service Dog Program. Wounded Warriors Canada, a registered Canadian charity, looks after the financial and charitable side of our work, including donations and funding. That lets our volunteers focus on matching veterans with the right dog and supporting them every step of the way.

What Is a Service Dog?

There is often confusion about what makes a true service dog. Every service dog is trained and tested for public access and task-trained to help their handler with a specific disability. That can include diabetic and seizure alerting, mobility assistance, autism support, hearing assistance, and OSI/PTSD intervention.

In Nova Scotia, service dogs and their handlers are protected under the Service Dog Act.

How It Works

Screening form

Complete our short online intake screening form so we can check the basic criteria.

Application package

If you meet the criteria, we'll contact you within a week to start your full application. Once it's complete, you join our wait list.

Train together

When funding and a dog are available (typically about 18 months), you travel to CARES in Concordia, Kansas, for 5 days of training with your new dog.

Go home as a team

Return home with a fully trained, certified service dog, with Paws Fur Thought behind you.

What's covered

Paws Fur Thought covers your service dog, your flights, and your accommodation in Concordia, and organizes rental vehicles for the drive from the Wichita airport (about 2 hours 15 minutes), carpooling where possible. Meals are not covered. New handlers travel in small groups, usually three, escorted by a Paws Fur Thought volunteer.

Our Partners

CARES, Inc. (Canine Assistance, Rehabilitation and Education Services) in Concordia, Kansas, trains and certifies every dog we place. CARES has placed nearly 2,200 assistance dogs since 1993.

Wounded Warriors Canada, a registered Canadian charity, supports Paws Fur Thought through its Service Dog Program and looks after the financial and charitable side of our work. Your donations to WWC help fund the dogs, training and travel for our veterans.
